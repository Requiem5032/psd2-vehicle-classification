Data Preprocessing & Class Selection

Our initial dataset included three vehicle categories. However, upon technical audit of the raw sensor data:

  • Bike & Car: High-quality signals with distinct vibration/magnetic signatures.
  • Bus: The captured imagery and sensor telemetry produced nonsensical patterns.
  • Decision: We removed the "Bus" class to maintain high training integrity.
